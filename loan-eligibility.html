How This Loan Affordability Calculator Works

The calculator applies two practical limits and uses the lower result. First, it checks how much room remains under your selected total EMI-to-income ratio. Second, it checks how much cash remains after essential household expenses and existing fixed obligations.

1. Maximum total EMI at your selected ratio

Monthly take-home income × selected ratio

2. Remaining room under the ratio

Maximum total EMI − existing EMIs and fixed obligations

3. Cash available after regular outgoings

Income − household expenses − existing fixed obligations

4. Estimated affordable new EMI

Lower of (2) and (3), but not below zero

Separating household expenses from existing EMIs helps avoid counting the same payment twice. Do not include an EMI in both fields.

Loan Affordability Calculation Example

Suppose your monthly take-home income is ₹60,000, household expenses are ₹30,000, existing EMIs and fixed obligations are ₹8,000, and your selected limit is 40%.

  • Maximum total EMI at 40%: ₹60,000 × 40% = ₹24,000
  • Remaining EMI room: ₹24,000 − ₹8,000 = ₹16,000
  • Cash after expenses and existing obligations: ₹60,000 − ₹30,000 − ₹8,000 = ₹22,000
  • Estimated affordable new EMI: the lower amount, ₹16,000
  • Estimated cash remaining after that EMI: ₹6,000

This example is illustrative. Keep a buffer for irregular bills, savings and emergencies rather than borrowing up to the calculated maximum.

What Is FOIR?

FOIR means Fixed Obligations to Income Ratio. It is one way lenders assess how much of a borrower's income is already committed to regular financial obligations. Lenders can differ in how they define eligible income, fixed obligations and acceptable ratios.

The percentage in this calculator is a planning limit you can adjust. It is not an official approval threshold. Your credit history, employment or business income, documentation, loan type and lender policy may also affect an application.

Loan Affordability FAQs

What does a loan affordability calculator estimate?

It estimates a monthly EMI budget by comparing take-home income, household expenses, existing EMIs and a user-selected EMI-to-income limit. It does not calculate a lender-approved loan amount.

How is my affordable new EMI calculated?

The estimate takes the lower of the remaining EMI room under your selected income ratio and the cash left after household expenses and existing fixed obligations.

What is FOIR?

FOIR means Fixed Obligations to Income Ratio. It compares relevant monthly fixed obligations with monthly income. Lenders may define income and obligations differently.

Is 40% a universal FOIR limit?

No. The default is an adjustable planning assumption, not a universal lender rule or a guarantee of approval. Choose a lower limit if you want a more cautious budget.

Should I include existing EMIs in household expenses?

No. Enter existing EMIs and fixed financial obligations in their separate field so they are not counted twice. Keep household expenses separate.

Can this calculator tell me the maximum loan amount?

No. It estimates a monthly payment budget. Use the InfoJano EMI Calculator to convert a chosen EMI, interest rate and tenure into an indicative loan amount and repayment estimate.
