How This Calculator Works

The calculator estimates the amount remaining after monthly expenses, then applies the percentage you choose to estimate a potential new EMI budget.

Actual loan eligibility depends on lender rules, credit history, existing debt, income stability and other factors.

Loan Affordability Example

A practical estimate starts with monthly take-home income, existing loan payments and essential expenses. The amount left over is not automatically the amount a lender will approve; household needs and unexpected costs also matter.

Use a cautious estimate

  • Enter income and existing obligations accurately.
  • Leave room for essential spending and emergencies.
  • Approval depends on lender checks, credit history, documents and loan policy.

Loan Affordability FAQs

What does loan affordability mean?

It is an estimate of a loan payment that may fit your income and expenses.

Does this guarantee loan approval?

No. Lenders assess credit history, income proof, existing debts, policy, and other eligibility criteria.

Why include existing expenses?

Existing commitments reduce the amount of monthly income available for a new loan payment.
