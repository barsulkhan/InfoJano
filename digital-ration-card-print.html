About Ration Card printing

West Bengal's Food & Supplies Department provides official e-Ration Card services and PDF downloads. InfoJano is an independent print-preparation utility; it does not issue, verify or modify ration cards.

How to prepare your Ration Card for printing

  1. Download your own e-Ration Card PDF from the official West Bengal Food & Supplies Department service.
  2. Open InfoJano ID Card Print Studio and choose Digital Ration Card.
  3. Upload the official e-Ration PDF. The current ration-card workflow is designed for the government e-Ration PDF layout; do not upload unrelated documents and expect automatic detection.
  4. Review the front and back areas in the crop and preview steps. Make sure the complete printed card content, QR code and edge details are retained.
  5. Check the A4 print layout and export PDF or JPG. Print using the previewed layout and confirm the final scale in your printer dialog.
Privacy and accuracy: Check that the full card, every printed detail and any QR code are visible before saving. InfoJano is a print-preparation utility only. It does not create official identity documents, change their details or replace the issuing authority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I download a West Bengal e-Ration Card?

Use the official West Bengal Food & Supplies Department website. The department provides e-Ration Card download services.

Can I upload any ration card photo?

The current dedicated Digital Ration Card workflow is designed around the official West Bengal e-Ration PDF layout. Other formats may need a different manual workflow and are not guaranteed.

Does InfoJano issue or update ration cards?

No. It only prepares an existing document for printing. Apply for or update a ration card through the official government portal.

Can I print both sides?

The tool supports a front-and-back print layout for the supported e-Ration PDF. Review both sides and the final print preview before exporting.
