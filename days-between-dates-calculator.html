How to calculate days between two dates

Choose a start date and an end date in the calculator. The result shows the elapsed calendar days between them, along with a useful years, months and days breakdown. Turn on the inclusive end-date option when you need to count both the start and end dates.

Calendar days vs. working days

Calendar days include every day on the calendar, including Saturdays, Sundays and holidays. Working-day calculations exclude the weekend days you select. You can also enter custom holiday dates when your schedule requires them.

Example: days between two dates

From January 1 to January 8 is 7 elapsed days. If you count both January 1 and January 8, the inclusive total is 8 calendar dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later date. For example, January 1 to January 8 is 7 elapsed days; an inclusive count of both dates is 8.

Does the calculator include the end date?

You can choose whether to include the end date. Keep it off for elapsed-day counting and turn it on when both endpoints should count.

Can I calculate weeks and months too?

Yes. The main calculator provides weeks and remaining days, plus a calendar-aware years, months and days breakdown.

Can I count weekdays only?

Yes. Use the working-day options to exclude selected weekend days and add any custom holiday dates that apply.
