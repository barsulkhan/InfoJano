📘  What Does Days Between Dates Mean?

It is the calendar-day distance between a starting date and an ending date. The total can be shown as days, weeks and days, or a calendar-aware duration.

📊  Calendar Days vs. Working Days

Calendar days include every day in the selected range. Working-day counts exclude selected weekends and holidays, so the totals are different.

❔  Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate days between two dates?

Enter the start and end dates in the date calculator and select Calculate to see the date difference.

Are both the start and end dates counted?

You can use the inclusive date option when you want both boundary dates included.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. Leap days are part of the calendar when they fall within the selected range.

Can I see the result in weeks and months?

The calculator provides a breakdown to help you understand the date range in days, weeks, months and years.
