How business days are counted

A common US workweek runs Monday through Friday, with Saturday and Sunday excluded. However, schedules vary. The InfoJano calculator lets you choose the weekend days that match your work pattern and add custom holiday dates.

Business days vs. calendar days

Calendar days include every date in the range. Business days count only dates that are not selected as weekends or entered holidays. Always check whether a contract, employer, bank or agency uses its own definition of a business day.

Planning with US holidays

The calculator supports custom holiday dates, so you can enter applicable US federal, state, company or personal closure dates. It does not automatically apply an official US holiday calendar; verify observed dates for the year you are planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a business day in the USA?

For many standard office schedules, a business day is Monday through Friday, excluding applicable holidays. Banks, courts, employers and contracts may use different calendars.

Are Saturdays and Sundays excluded?

You can select the weekend days to exclude. Saturday and Sunday are common defaults for a Monday-to-Friday workweek.

Does it automatically exclude US federal holidays?

No. You can add holiday dates manually. Check the official observed date for each holiday and year before entering it.

Can I count business days between two dates?

Yes. Enter the start and end dates, configure weekend days and add any custom holidays, then calculate the working-day total.
