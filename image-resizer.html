1How to Resize an Image

  1. Upload your image.

  4. Tap Resize Image, then download your new image.

✦Tips for Best Results

  • Keep aspect ratio to avoid distortion.
  • Use JPG for photos and PNG for transparency.
  • For website use, 800 to 1200 px width is often enough.
  • Higher quality usually means a larger file.
  • Print size also depends on DPI and printer settings.

Image Resizing Example

If a website asks for a 600 × 600 pixel image, enter those exact dimensions. Keep the aspect ratio on to avoid stretching. For print, check both physical size and DPI.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize an image?

Choose an image, enter the required dimensions or select a preset, then tap Resize Image.

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Changing dimensions or compressing an image can affect quality. Use a higher quality setting when image detail matters.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The image is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to InfoJano.
