How to create it

  1. Open the QR Maker with Email mode selected.
  2. Enter the recipient email address.
  3. Optionally add a subject and a short message.
  4. Generate and scan-test the QR code. Confirm the mail app shows the correct recipient and draft.

Safety and quality checks

  • Check the content and destination carefully before generating or sharing the QR code.
  • Scan the finished QR code with another phone and confirm the decoded information is correct.
  • Use strong contrast, a clear quiet border and a suitable print size.
  • Do not encode passwords, private data or payment instructions unless you understand who can access the QR content.

A QR code stores information; it does not guarantee that a link, phone number, email or payment destination is trustworthy. Review the result before taking action.

Frequently asked questions

Does scanning send the email automatically?

No. It opens or prepares a draft in a compatible mail app. The person scanning must review and send it.

Can I add a subject and message?

Yes. Both are optional fields in the Email QR mode.

Can I use more than one recipient?

The current mode is designed around one recipient address. Use a normal email link or a web form for more complex routing.
