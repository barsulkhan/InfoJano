How to create a email qr code

  1. Enter the recipient email address and check every character.
  2. Add an optional subject and short message.
  3. Choose Email in the QR Maker, generate the code and test it with a phone that has a mail app configured.

Where can you use it?

Customer enquiry desks
Event feedback and registration
Product support and warranty cards
Printed contact information

Choose a clear purpose and make sure the destination or information is ready before printing the QR code.

Tips for a reliable QR code

  • Use an inbox that is monitored and appropriate for public enquiries.
  • Avoid putting private information in a pre-filled message.
  • Scanning prepares a draft; the user should review it before sending.
  • Test the QR with the email apps commonly used by your audience.

A QR code stores information; it does not prove that a link, phone number, location or payment destination is trustworthy. Review the decoded result before taking action.

Frequently asked questions

Does scanning send the email automatically?

No. It opens or prepares a draft in a compatible mail app. The person scanning must review and send it.

Can I add a subject and message?

Yes. The Email mode supports optional subject and message fields.

Will it work without an email app?

The phone needs a compatible mail handler or may offer to open a webmail option. Behaviour varies by device.
