Features

Indian and International grouping, INR and USD currency, decimals, cheque-ready wording, ordinal numbers, Roman numerals, reverse English words-to-number, bulk conversion, speech, print, TXT and CSV export. Conversion is performed locally in your browser.

Number system guide

Indian numbering uses thousand, lakh and crore. International numbering uses thousand, million and billion. Select the format that matches the document or audience.

Frequently asked questions

Can I write cheque amounts in words?

Yes. Select Currency Amount or Cheque Format and choose INR or USD.

Can I convert multiple numbers?

Yes. Enter one number per line in the bulk conversion box.

Can I convert words back to numbers?

Yes. Enter supported English number words in the reverse converter.
