What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening measure calculated from a person's weight and height. It can help provide a general indication of weight status, but it does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight in kilograms ÷ (Height in metres × Height in metres)

Adult BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9: Healthy weight range
  • 25 to 29.9: Overweight
  • 30 or above: Obesity range

These categories are general screening guidelines for adults and may not apply to children, teenagers, pregnant people, or every population. BMI is not a medical diagnosis.
