What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening measure calculated from a person's weight and height. It can help provide a general indication of weight status, but it does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight in kilograms ÷ (Height in metres × Height in metres)

Adult BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9: Healthy weight range
  • 25 to 29.9: Overweight
  • 30 or above: Obesity range

These categories are general screening guidelines for adults and may not apply to children, teenagers, pregnant people, or every population. BMI is not a medical diagnosis.

BMI Example and How to Read the Result

For example, a weight of 70 kg and a height of 1.75 m gives a BMI of 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75), or about 22.9. This is a screening estimate, not a diagnosis.

Get a more useful estimate

  • Measure height without shoes and enter centimetres.
  • Use a recent weight measurement and enter kilograms.
  • Interpret BMI with age, health history and other factors in mind; children require age-specific interpretation.

BMI Calculator FAQs

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

What units should I enter?

Enter weight in kilograms and height in centimetres in this calculator.
