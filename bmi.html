What Is Body Mass Index (BMI)?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening measure that compares a person's weight with their height. It can help adults understand a general weight category, but it does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, fat distribution or overall health.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ [Height (m) × Height (m)]

Example: If you weigh 70 kg and are 175 cm tall, the calculation is 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.9 (approximately).

These are general screening categories for adults aged 20 and over. BMI for children and teens needs age- and sex-specific interpretation. BMI is not a diagnosis.

Estimated Healthy Weight Range

For adults, the commonly used BMI range of 18.5–24.9 can be used to estimate a weight range for a given height. This is a general reference, not a personal target or a substitute for clinical assessment. Your calculator result displays this estimate after you enter your measurements.

How to Use This BMI Calculator

  2. Enter your height in centimetres (cm).

  4. Select an optional daily activity level to tailor the general movement tips.
  5. Select Calculate BMI to view your result, adult category guidance and estimated healthy weight range.

For a more consistent estimate, measure your height without shoes and use a recent weight measurement.

What Are the Limitations of BMI?

BMI is useful as a quick screening measure, but it has important limitations:

  • It does not directly measure body fat or muscle mass.
  • It does not show where body fat is stored.
  • It may not reflect differences in age, pregnancy, muscle build or individual health conditions.
  • It should not be used to classify children and teenagers with adult BMI categories.

Consider BMI alongside other information such as waist measurement, blood pressure, medical history and advice from a qualified healthcare professional. If you are concerned about your weight or health, seek individual guidance.

BMI Calculator FAQs

What is a good BMI?

For most adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly described as the healthy-weight range. BMI is only a screening measure and should be considered with other health information.

How do I calculate my BMI?

Divide your weight in kilograms by your height in metres multiplied by itself. This calculator performs the calculation after you enter your weight and height.

What is my ideal weight for my height?

There is no single ideal weight for everyone. This calculator shows an estimated adult weight range based on the standard BMI range of 18.5–24.9, but personal health factors also matter.

Is BMI the same for men and women?

The standard adult BMI categories are generally the same for adult men and women. However, BMI does not capture every difference in body composition or individual health.

Does BMI measure body fat?

No. BMI uses height and weight and does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass or fat distribution.

Can children and teenagers use this BMI calculator?

This page does not calculate child or teen BMI percentiles. For people aged 2–19, BMI needs age- and sex-specific interpretation. Do not use adult BMI categories to classify children or teenagers.

How often should I check my BMI?

There is no single schedule that suits everyone. If you monitor BMI, focus on longer-term trends rather than day-to-day changes, and discuss concerns with a healthcare professional.

Is this BMI calculator free?

Yes. The InfoJano BMI Calculator is free to use. Enter weight in kilograms and height in centimetres to get a general BMI estimate.
