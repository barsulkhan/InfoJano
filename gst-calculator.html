What Is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax applied to many goods and services in India. The applicable rate depends on the item or service.

GST Formula

GST Amount = Price Before GST × GST Rate ÷ 100.

Final Price = Price Before GST + GST Amount.

This calculator adds GST to a pre-tax price. It does not determine the legally applicable GST rate for a product or service.
