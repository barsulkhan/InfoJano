Word Converter – FAQs

How do I convert a Word document to PDF?

Choose your DOCX file, check the page preview, select the pages you need and tap Convert.

Can I convert only selected Word pages to PDF?

Yes. Enter page numbers or ranges such as 2-5, 8, 10-12. Only selected pages are included in the chosen output format.

Will the PDF keep my Word formatting?

The converter uses browser-based DOCX rendering. Common text, tables, images and page breaks are supported, but complex layouts and fonts may differ from Microsoft Word. Review the preview before downloading.

Does this tool support old .doc files?

Not yet. Open the file in Microsoft Word or another compatible editor and save a copy as .docx first.

Are my Word documents uploaded to a server?

The document is processed in your browser. It is not uploaded to an InfoJano processing server. Conversion libraries are loaded from public CDNs.

Will the result look exactly like Microsoft Word?

Browser rendering can differ from desktop Word, especially with uncommon fonts, complex layouts, floating objects and advanced page settings. Always check the preview before downloading.
