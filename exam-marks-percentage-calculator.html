Exam Marks Percentage Calculator: Formula

Marks percentage = (Marks obtained ÷ Total marks) × 100

Worked example

You scored 425 out of 500. Percentage = (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the marks you obtained.
  2. Enter the total or maximum marks.
  3. Calculate to find your exam percentage.

Common questions

How do I calculate percentage from marks?

Divide the marks obtained by the total marks and multiply by 100.

What is 425 out of 500 as a percentage?

425 ÷ 500 × 100 = 85%.

Can I calculate percentage for multiple subjects?

Use the combined marks obtained and combined total marks for an overall percentage, if your exam rules allow totals to be combined.
