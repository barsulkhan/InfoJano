How to create multiple QR codes

  1. Paste one website link or text value on each line, or choose CSV input.
  2. Tap Generate QR Codes to create the batch.
  3. Check the preview and download each PNG file.
  4. Scan each important code with a phone before printing or publishing.

What can I use bulk QR codes for?

Create QR codes for product pages, inventory labels, event tickets, classroom resources, business locations, contact details and lists of website links. Each QR code contains the exact value you provide.

Frequently asked questions

Can I generate QR codes from Excel?

Save your spreadsheet as a CSV or TXT file and upload it, or paste CSV text into the box. Direct XLSX upload is not supported.

Can I download all QR codes as a ZIP?

Yes. After generating the batch, choose Download all as ZIP to save the PNG files together.

Can I download a print-ready sheet?

Yes. Choose Download A4 PDF sheet to arrange up to six QR codes per page.

Is my list uploaded?

QR generation happens locally in your browser. The list is not sent to an InfoJano server by this tool.

How many QR codes can I create at once?

This version supports up to 50 values per batch to keep the page responsive on mobile devices.
