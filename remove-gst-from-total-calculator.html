Formula

Base amount = Total including GST ÷ (1 + GST rate ÷ 100).

Example: ₹1,180 including 18% GST = ₹1,000 before tax and ₹180 GST.

How to use

Enter the total bill amount and GST rate, then select Remove GST. This free tool works in your browser.
