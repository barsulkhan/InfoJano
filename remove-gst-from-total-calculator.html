Remove GST from Total Calculator: Formula

Base amount = Inclusive total × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate); GST amount = Total − Base amount

Worked example

₹1,180 including 18% GST: base amount = 1,180 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹1,000. GST included = ₹180.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the total amount that already includes GST.
  2. Enter the GST rate shown on the bill or applicable to the item.
  3. Select Remove GST to calculate the base amount and GST included.

Common questions

How do I remove 18% GST from a total amount?

Divide the GST-inclusive total by 1.18 to find the base amount. Subtract the base amount from the total to find GST included.

What is the base price of ₹1,180 including 18% GST?

The base price is ₹1,000 and the GST included is ₹180.

Can I simply subtract 18% from the GST-inclusive total?

No. GST is calculated on the base price, not on the inclusive total. Use the reverse GST formula to extract it correctly.
