Original Price Before Discount Calculator: Formula

Original price = Discounted price ÷ (1 − Discount percentage ÷ 100)

Worked example

A product costs 800 after a 20% discount. Original price = 800 ÷ 0.80 = 1,000.

How to use this calculator

Common questions

How do I find the original price after a discount?

Divide the sale price by 1 minus the discount rate as a decimal. For a 20% discount, divide by 0.80.

Is the original price the same as MRP?

It can be the marked price or listed original price, if that is the amount from which the discount was applied.

Can I calculate the discount amount too?

This page focuses on finding the original price. Use the main Percentage Calculator's discount mode to calculate the discount amount and sale price.
