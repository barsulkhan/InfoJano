Ayushman Bharat and Aadhaar cards • JPG, PNG, PDF • Front and Back print setup

1Choose card type

PDF pages and photos are supported. You can upload two separate photos, or one PDF/scan containing the front and back. Files are processed in this browser; they are not intentionally uploaded to an InfoJano server.

Choose a PDF or one or more photos to begin.

2Crop and review card sides

Ayushman JPG/PNG keeps the approved automatic split. For Ayushman PDF files and scanned PDF copies, the original page is kept intact: choose that page below, crop only the complete card for Front, then crop the real Back from the same or another page. Do not use the whole page or an instruction panel as a card. The final card is fitted to 85 × 55 mm at 300 DPI with a thin black cut border.

Step 1 — Crop the complete card

Choose the original PDF page below. Drag around only the full card rectangle, including every edge, all text, both colour bands, the portrait and the complete QR code. Press “Use crop as Front”. For a genuine back side, choose its source page and repeat with “Use crop as Back”. Do not select the instruction panel or the whole PDF page.

Select a source above to crop it.

3Set card size, add border and review

Processing order: crop the card, resize it to the selected physical dimensions at the chosen DPI, then draw a thin cut line on the exact outer edge of that fixed-size box. The line does not add to or subtract from the selected card dimensions. Check both previews and print at Actual size / 100%.

Print layout preview will appear here.

4Download print-ready PDF or JPG

Print at “Actual size” / 100% scale. Turn off “Fit to page” if exact physical dimensions are important. This first version uses a manual front/back selection to avoid silently printing the wrong side.
