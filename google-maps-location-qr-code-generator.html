How to create a google maps location qr code

  1. Find the exact place in a map app and copy its latitude and longitude, or prepare a Google Maps share URL.
  2. Choose Location in the QR Maker and enter the required coordinates.
  3. Generate the QR code and scan it to confirm that the map opens at the intended point.

Where can you use it?

Shop and office directions
Wedding and event invitations
Tourist attractions and visitor information
Delivery and pickup points

Choose a clear purpose and make sure the destination or information is ready before printing the QR code.

Tips for a reliable QR code

  • Verify the map pin at street level before printing.
  • For a private or hard-to-find venue, check that the location can be opened by visitors.
  • The current Location mode uses latitude and longitude; a Maps share URL can also be encoded using Link mode.
  • Test on a phone with its location/map app available.

A QR code stores information; it does not prove that a link, phone number, location or payment destination is trustworthy. Review the decoded result before taking action.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find latitude and longitude?

In a map app, select or long-press the exact point and copy the coordinates shown in its place details.

Can I use a place name instead?

The dedicated Location mode uses coordinates. You can encode a Google Maps share link with Link / URL mode.

Will it open Google Maps?

The QR stores a Maps URL. A phone may open the Maps app or a browser depending on installed apps and settings.
