How to Calculate Elapsed Time

Learn the time-difference formula with clear examples for everyday intervals, overnight times, unpaid breaks and longer date-and-time spans.

Elapsed time is the amount of time that passes between a starting moment and an ending moment. To calculate it, subtract the start time from the end time. If the interval continues past midnight, account for the next day before subtracting.

How to calculate time between two times

  1. Write both clock readings in 24-hour format.
  2. Convert each time to minutes after midnight: hours × 60 + minutes.
  3. Subtract the start total from the end total.
  4. If the event ends on the next day, add 1,440 minutes to the end total before subtracting.
  5. Convert the result into hours and remaining minutes. If seconds matter, use seconds throughout.
Elapsed minutes = End minutes − Start minutes
For an overnight interval: Elapsed minutes = End minutes + 1,440 − Start minutes
Example 1: 9:15 AM to 5:45 PM

09:15 = 555 minutes; 17:45 = 1,065 minutes. Difference: 1,065 − 555 = 510 minutes = 8 hours 30 minutes.

Example 2: 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM

22:00 = 1,320 minutes; 06:00 = 360 minutes. Overnight: 360 + 1,440 − 1,320 = 480 minutes = 8 hours.

How to calculate elapsed time across midnight

When an event starts late at night and ends after midnight, the end clock reading is smaller even though the end moment is later. Treat the end as belonging to the following day. For example, 11:30 PM to 2:15 AM is 2 hours 45 minutes, not a negative duration.

This clock-only rule assumes the interval crosses midnight when the end time is earlier than the start time. If the times are equal, the result is treated as zero in a clock-only calculation. To represent a full 24 hours or a longer period, enter the actual dates in a date-and-time calculator.

How to subtract a break from elapsed time

First calculate the gross interval. Convert that duration to minutes, subtract the unpaid break minutes, and convert the remaining minutes back to hours and minutes.

Net time = Gross elapsed minutes − Unpaid break minutes

Example: 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM is 510 minutes (8 hours 30 minutes). Subtract a 30-minute unpaid break: 510 − 30 = 480 minutes, or 8 net hours. Only subtract a break when it should be excluded from the duration you are measuring.

How to convert hours and minutes to decimal hours

Divide the minutes by 60, then add that fraction to the whole hours. Decimal hours are useful for spreadsheets and time records, but the digits after the decimal are a fraction of an hour—not extra minutes.

Decimal hours = Hours + (Minutes ÷ 60)

For example, 8 hours 30 minutes is 8.5 decimal hours—not 8.30.

When to use date and time or add/subtract mode

A pair of clock times is suitable for a same-day or overnight interval. If the period spans multiple calendar days, use actual start and end dates as well as times. If you know a starting clock time and want to find a later or earlier clock reading after a duration, use an add/subtract time calculation instead.

  • Time Difference: measure the interval between two clock times, including overnight rollover and optional break deduction.
  • Add / Subtract Time: add or subtract hours, minutes and seconds from a starting clock time.
  • Date + Time: calculate a duration using both start and end dates and times, including periods longer than 24 hours.

Common uses for an elapsed time calculator

People use elapsed-time calculations to measure study sessions, exercise, travel, appointments, event durations, machine run time and work intervals. For payroll or attendance, follow the applicable employer rules for rounding, breaks and paid time; a general time calculator does not determine legal pay entitlements.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the time between two times?

Convert both times to minutes after midnight and subtract the start from the end. If the interval crosses midnight, add 1,440 minutes to the end first.

How do I calculate time from 9 AM to 5 PM?

5:00 PM is 17:00 in 24-hour time. 17:00 − 09:00 = 8 hours.

How do I calculate an overnight time difference?

When the event continues into the next day, add 24 hours to the end time before subtracting. For example, 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8 hours.

How do I subtract a 30-minute break?

Calculate the full interval in minutes, subtract 30, then convert the remaining minutes into hours and minutes.

Is 8 hours 30 minutes the same as 8.30 hours?

No. It is 8.5 decimal hours because 30 ÷ 60 = 0.5. The value 8.30 decimal hours is 8 hours and 18 minutes.

Can I calculate time across multiple dates?

Yes. Enter both dates and clock times in the Date + Time mode of the calculator. Clock-only inputs are intended for intervals up to one day.

Does the calculator decide paid hours or overtime?

No. It calculates time durations and optional break deductions. Employer policies and local rules determine paid time, rounding and overtime.
