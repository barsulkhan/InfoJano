About Aadhaar Card printing

UIDAI describes e-Aadhaar as a password-protected, digitally signed electronic copy and states that it is equally valid as the physical Aadhaar copy. InfoJano does not issue Aadhaar or verify identity; it only helps prepare a document already downloaded by its owner for printing.

How to prepare your Aadhaar Card for printing

  1. Download your e-Aadhaar from UIDAI's official myAadhaar portal. Keep the original PDF secure; it may be password-protected.
  2. Open InfoJano ID Card Print Studio and choose Aadhaar Card.
  3. Upload the PDF or image. The page supports Aadhaar PDF/photo workflows; review the detected card sides and use the manual crop canvas if the complete card boundary is not selected correctly.
  4. Keep the full card border, all printed text and the QR code inside the crop. Do not crop out security or verification details.
  5. Review the front/back previews, choose the required paper layout and export PDF or JPG. Check the print preview before printing.
Privacy and accuracy: Check that the full card, every printed detail and any QR code are visible before saving. InfoJano is a print-preparation utility only. It does not create official identity documents, change their details or replace the issuing authority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I download e-Aadhaar?

Use UIDAI's official myAadhaar portal or official Aadhaar app. InfoJano does not provide Aadhaar downloads.

Is e-Aadhaar valid?

UIDAI states that e-Aadhaar is equally valid as the physical copy. Check UIDAI's current guidance for official validity details.

Can I crop the Aadhaar PDF here?

Yes. Upload the PDF and review the card selection. If the detected region is incomplete, use the manual crop canvas and keep the complete QR code and all edges.

Does InfoJano change Aadhaar details?

No. It only prepares the file for printing. It cannot update, correct, verify or issue Aadhaar details.
