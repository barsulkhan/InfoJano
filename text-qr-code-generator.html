Safety and quality checks

  • Check the content and destination carefully before generating or sharing the QR code.
  • Scan the finished QR code with another phone and confirm the decoded information is correct.
  • Use strong contrast, a clear quiet border and a suitable print size.
  • Do not encode passwords, private data or payment instructions unless you understand who can access the QR content.

A QR code stores information; it does not guarantee that a link, phone number, email or payment destination is trustworthy. Review the result before taking action.

Frequently asked questions

Does a text QR code need internet?

The stored text itself can be decoded without opening a website. A phone may still need a QR scanner.

Can I store a long document?

QR codes have limited data capacity. Keep the text concise; use a URL QR code for long documents or web pages.

Can I edit the text after printing?

No. The text is encoded in the QR image. Generate and print a new code if the information changes.
