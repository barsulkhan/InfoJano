How to create a text qr code

  1. Prepare the exact text you want people to read.
  2. Choose Text in the QR Maker and paste your message.
  3. Generate the code, scan it on another phone and check line breaks and spelling.

Where can you use it?

Product labels and equipment instructions
Classroom notes and quick reference cards
Event directions and short announcements
Inventory tags and offline information

Choose a clear purpose and make sure the destination or information is ready before printing the QR code.

Tips for a reliable QR code

  • Keep the message concise; QR codes have a finite data capacity.
  • Do not encode passwords, private records or confidential information.
  • Use a URL QR code for long documents or frequently updated information.
  • Check punctuation and line breaks after scanning.

A QR code stores information; it does not prove that a link, phone number, location or payment destination is trustworthy. Review the decoded result before taking action.

Frequently asked questions

Does a text QR code need internet?

The stored text can be decoded without opening a website. A phone still needs a camera or QR scanning app.

Can I store a long document?

Long content makes a denser code that can be harder to scan. Use a URL to a document for lengthy material.

Can I edit the text after printing?

No. The text is encoded in the image. Generate and print a new QR code when the message changes.
