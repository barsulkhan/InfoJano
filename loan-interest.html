ⓘ Simple Interest Formula

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where,

  • SI = Simple Interest amount
  • P = Principal (loan amount)
  • R = Annual interest rate (%)
  • T = Time in years

Key Points About Loan Interest

  • This calculator uses the simple interest formula on the original principal.
  • Interest is calculated from the annual rate and the selected time period.
  • Total repayment is the original principal plus estimated simple interest.
  • Many bank loans use reducing-balance interest with monthly EMIs, which is different.
  • Check processing fees, insurance, penalties and other charges in your loan agreement.

Simple Interest Calculation Example

Suppose you borrow ₹1,00,000 at an annual simple interest rate of 10% for 2 years.

  • Interest = ₹1,00,000 × 10 × 2 ÷ 100 = ₹20,000
  • Total repayment = ₹1,00,000 + ₹20,000 = ₹1,20,000

Use the calculator above with your own amount, rate and tenure to get an estimate. If your lender calculates interest on a reducing balance, use the EMI Calculator instead.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is simple interest on a loan?

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal amount for the selected period. It does not add previously accrued interest to the principal.

How do I calculate interest on a loan?

For simple interest, multiply the principal by the annual interest rate and the time in years, then divide by 100. The formula is SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100.

Is simple interest the same as EMI interest?

No. Many EMI loans use a reducing-balance method. Interest is calculated on the outstanding principal, which generally decreases as instalments are paid.

Can I calculate loan interest per month?

This calculator accepts tenure in years or months. When you select months, it converts the period into years before applying the simple interest formula.

Does this calculator include processing fees?

No. It estimates simple interest and principal repayment only. Check your lender's loan agreement for processing fees, taxes, insurance and other charges.

Which calculator should I use for monthly loan instalments?

Use the InfoJano EMI Calculator to estimate monthly instalments, total interest and repayment for a reducing-balance loan.
