📘  What Does Date Duration Mean?

Date duration is the calendar time between a starting date and an ending date. It can be expressed as total days, weeks and days, or complete years, months and remaining days.

📊  How Calendar Duration Works

Month lengths and leap years matter. A calendar-aware duration is not always the same as dividing a total number of days by 30 or 365.

❔  Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate duration between two dates?

Enter both dates in the calculator and review the calendar-based duration breakdown.

Is date duration the same as total days?

No. Total days is one measure; years, months and remaining days is a calendar-based breakdown.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Leap days are considered when they fall within the selected date range.

Can I include the final date?

Use the inclusive end-date option when the final date should be counted.
