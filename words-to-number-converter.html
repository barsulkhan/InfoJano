About Words to Number Converter

Turn common English number phrases into digits. Enter phrases such as one hundred twenty five or two million three hundred thousand.

How to use

  1. Enter a supported English number phrase, such as “one hundred twenty five”.
  2. Choose Indian or International digit grouping.
  3. Select plain digits, currency or cheque-style output.
  4. Convert, then copy the numeric result.
