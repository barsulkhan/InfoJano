Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

InfoJano (https://infojano.com) provides browser-based online tools and informational features. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website or use its tools, why it is processed, and the choices that may be available to you. InfoJano is also distributed through Android and Windows desktop wrapper applications that load hosted InfoJano web pages; the same web-page features and relevant hosting, analytics, advertising, consent and third-party requests may therefore apply when you use those applications. The Android wrapper also supports system file selection and saving generated files to the device.

Who operates InfoJano: InfoJano is operated by Abdul Barik, an individual based in West Bengal, India. For privacy questions or requests, you can contact the operator at infojanowebsite@gmail.com.

Important: This policy describes the website features and integrations identified in the current repository review. Some third-party services, live network behavior, advertising partners, and retention periods depend on configuration or providers and have not all been independently verified. We will update this notice when that review is complete.

1. Information processed when you visit

When you load a page, your browser and the website's hosting, security, analytics, advertising, or embedded-service providers may process technical information such as your IP address, requested page and URL, date and time of the request, browser and device characteristics, language, referring page, and diagnostic or interaction events. The exact information depends on the service and your browser settings.

InfoJano's reviewed source contains Google Analytics 4 measurement (measurement ID G-P8D7XRGVEF) on 24 identified pages. This source finding does not establish that every page fires an analytics request, or that a request fires before or after consent in every browser or app session. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies and process usage, device and network information to provide measurement and reporting. Live firing and consent behavior are still being checked.

2. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

Cookies and browser storage may be used for analytics, advertising, consent choices, language preferences, and optional tool features. Examples identified in the source include a saved language preference; Countdown Studio settings such as a countdown name, target date/time, timezone, message, theme and optional media references; a temporary QR Scanner-to-QR Maker handoff in session storage; optional work-hours entries; optional BMI history; birthday reminder preferences; a saved Word Converter service endpoint; up to eight percentage-calculation history entries; and up to five elapsed-time history entries. BMI history may include weight, height, date and BMI values. Work Hours Calculator entries are saved only if you enable its “Remember my entries on this device” option. Some tool data is stored only when you choose a save or remember feature. Use the relevant tool's clear, reset or opt-out control where provided, or clear site data through your browser settings.

Google AdSense and other advertising technology may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to deliver ads, measure ad performance, limit repeated ads, detect invalid activity, or personalize ads where permitted and where your choices allow it. Third parties may place or read cookies in your browser as a result of ad serving. The exact vendors and technologies can vary by auction, region, and account configuration. Google publishes a current ad technology partner list and a certified external vendor directory; the vendors selected for this publisher account must still be checked against its live AdSense and consent settings.

4. Third-party services and external content

Source review has identified the following services or external resources in some InfoJano features. A provider receives information when your browser or a feature makes a request to it. Its own privacy policy and terms govern its processing:

  • Google Analytics, Google AdSense and Google Privacy & Messaging: analytics, advertising, and consent management. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube Data API and YouTube embedded player: Countdown Studio can request public channel statistics when a visitor supplies a channel reference and API key, and can embed YouTube video content using YouTube’s Privacy Enhanced Mode domain (youtube-nocookie.com). The embed still makes a request to YouTube; Google says views in this mode are not used to personalize the viewer’s YouTube experience or ads outside the site. See Google Privacy Policy and YouTube’s Privacy Enhanced Mode information.
  • ipapi.co: Age Calculator requests an IP-based location estimate to support a local-time feature. See ipapi privacy policy.
  • Exchange-rate services: Currency Converter requests current rates from ExchangeRate-API terms and privacy information and historical reference rates from Frankfurter.
  • Wikimedia services: some date/event features request public information from Wikipedia and Wikidata. See Wikimedia Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Worker and iLoveAPI: the optional high-fidelity Word conversion path, when available and after the visitor chooses the external-processing option, sends a DOCX file from the browser to an InfoJano-configured Worker designed to forward it to iLoveAPI’s Office-to-PDF service in the India region. The file is therefore processed outside the visitor’s device by the Worker and provider. The Worker source reviewed for this policy allows the GitHub Pages origin; production custom-domain availability and CORS configuration have not been confirmed. The provider materials reviewed contain inconsistent file-retention statements: the API-specific terms state a maximum of 48 hours, while general terms and security materials state 2 hours. We do not make a single guaranteed retention-time claim; check the provider’s current terms for your API plan. Do not use this option for confidential documents unless you accept the external processing and provider terms. See iLovePDF/iLoveAPI Privacy Policy and iLoveAPI Terms.
  • Public CDNs, fonts and assets: source review identified cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg, Google Fonts and FlagCDN for scripts, fonts, PDF/document libraries, barcode/QR support and currency flag images. A request to one of these providers can disclose technical request data such as your IP address, browser details and requested resource.

This list reflects integrations found in source review and may not be exhaustive. It does not mean that every listed service is contacted on every page or app session. External providers may change their services and policies.

5. How our tools handle content you enter

Many InfoJano tools perform calculations or file transformations in your browser. For example, the image compression, image-to-PDF, PDF-to-JPG, and several calculator features are designed to process input locally rather than upload it to an InfoJano file-processing server. Browser extensions, embedded resources, analytics, or other third-party requests may still operate while a page is open.

Some optional features behave differently:

  • Work Hours Calculator entries are saved in your browser's local storage only if you enable its remember option.
  • BMI history is saved in local storage only when you choose to save it. It may contain weight, height, date, and BMI values. Treat this as sensitive information on a shared device.
  • Age Calculator reminder preferences may be stored in local storage. The reviewed source did not establish notification delivery for every birthday/countdown tool. Birthday Countdown Calculator source mentions notifications, but the reviewed page source did not contain a browser Notification API call, permission request or reminder-storage implementation; do not assume that page currently delivers browser notifications.
  • Countdown Studio's optional public channel-statistics feature uses a YouTube Data API key entered by the visitor in the browser. Restrict your own key as recommended by Google and do not share private credentials.
  • The optional high-fidelity Word Converter route, when available and correctly configured for the page you are using, sends the selected DOCX file to an external conversion provider through a Worker after an explicit in-page consent action. Production custom-domain availability, CORS and Worker logging/retention configuration have not been confirmed. The provider's own processing and retention terms apply; its published materials reviewed for this policy contain inconsistent retention statements, so check the current terms for your API plan.
  • QR Scanner can request camera access only after you choose Start Camera. Decoding of an uploaded QR image is performed in the browser using a third-party library; camera frames are not intentionally sent to an InfoJano processing server by the inspected page code.
  • The Android wrapper uses a WebView to load hosted InfoJano pages and supports system file selection and saving generated files to the device's Downloads area. The reviewed Android manifest did not declare camera, microphone, location, contacts or notification permissions. A web page may still request browser/WebView capabilities where supported. The Windows desktop wrapper loads hosted InfoJano pages and opens external links in the system browser. These descriptions are based on source review, not device-level runtime testing.
