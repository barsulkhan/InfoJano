1. Information processed when you visit

When you load a page, your browser and the website's hosting, security, analytics, advertising, or embedded-service providers may process technical information such as your IP address, requested page and URL, date and time of the request, browser and device characteristics, language, referring page, and diagnostic or interaction events. The exact information depends on the service and your browser settings.

InfoJano's source code includes Google Analytics 4 measurement (measurement ID G-P8D7XRGVEF) on multiple pages. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies and process usage and device information to provide measurement and reporting. Live firing and consent behavior are still being checked.

2. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

Cookies and browser storage may be used for analytics, advertising, consent choices, language preferences, and optional tool features. Examples identified in the source include a saved language preference, optional work-hours entries, optional BMI history, birthday reminder preferences, and a saved Word Converter service endpoint. Some tool data is stored only when you choose a save or remember feature; you can generally clear browser storage through your browser settings or the relevant tool's clear control.

Google AdSense and other advertising technology may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to deliver ads, measure ad performance, limit repeated ads, detect invalid activity, or personalize ads where permitted and where your choices allow it. Third parties may place or read cookies in your browser as a result of ad serving. The exact vendors and technologies can vary by auction, region, and account configuration. Google publishes a current ad technology partner list and a certified external vendor directory; the vendors selected for this publisher account must still be checked against its live AdSense and consent settings.

Google and its partners may use information about visits to this and other websites to provide, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to your settings and applicable consent requirements. The advertising technology providers eligible to receive data can vary with the account configuration, region, and auction; Google maintains a current list of ad technology partners. You can use the privacy or cookie settings control shown on the site, if displayed, to revisit available choices. The live visibility and behavior of that control are still being verified. Browser settings can also be used to block or delete cookies, although some site features may not work as intended.

4. Third-party services and external content

Source review has identified the following services or external resources in some InfoJano features. A provider receives information when your browser or a feature makes a request to it. Its own privacy policy and terms govern its processing:

  • Google Analytics, Google AdSense and Google Privacy & Messaging: analytics, advertising, and consent management. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube Data API and YouTube embedded player: Countdown Studio can request public channel statistics when a visitor supplies a channel reference and API key, and can embed YouTube video content using YouTube’s Privacy Enhanced Mode domain (youtube-nocookie.com). The embed still makes a request to YouTube; Google says views in this mode are not used to personalize the viewer’s YouTube experience or ads outside the site. See Google Privacy Policy and YouTube’s Privacy Enhanced Mode information.
  • ipapi.co: Age Calculator requests an IP-based location estimate to support a local-time feature. See ipapi privacy policy.
  • Exchange-rate services: Currency Converter requests current rates from ExchangeRate-API terms and privacy information and historical reference rates from Frankfurter.
  • Wikimedia services: some date/event features request public information from Wikipedia and Wikidata. See Wikimedia Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Worker and iLoveAPI: the optional high-fidelity Word conversion path sends a DOCX file to a Worker configured for InfoJano, which is designed to forward it to iLoveAPI’s Office-to-PDF service in the India region after the visitor explicitly chooses that path. The Worker source currently allows the GitHub Pages origin only; availability from the infojano.com custom domain has not been verified. iLoveAPI’s current API terms state that files processed through its API are deleted from its servers within a maximum of 48 hours. This is the provider’s stated file-retention period, not a retention promise by InfoJano; provider terms may change. Do not use this option for confidential documents unless you are comfortable with that external processing. See iLovePDF/iLoveAPI Privacy Policy and iLoveAPI Terms.
  • Public CDNs, fonts and assets: source review identified cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg, Google Fonts and FlagCDN for scripts, fonts, PDF/document libraries, barcode/QR support and currency flag images. A request to one of these providers can disclose technical request data such as your IP address, browser details and requested resource.

This list reflects integrations found in source review and may not be exhaustive. We are continuing to check every page, shared script, live configuration, and advertising vendor list. External providers may change their services and policies.

5. How our tools handle content you enter

Many InfoJano tools perform calculations or file transformations in your browser. For example, the image compression, image-to-PDF, PDF-to-JPG, and several calculator features are designed to process input locally rather than upload it to an InfoJano file-processing server. Browser extensions, embedded resources, analytics, or other third-party requests may still operate while a page is open.

Some optional features behave differently:

  • Work Hours Calculator entries are saved in your browser's local storage only if you enable its remember option.
  • BMI history is saved in local storage only when you choose to save it. It may contain weight, height, date, and BMI values. Treat this as sensitive information on a shared device.
  • Age reminder preferences may be stored in local storage, and browser notifications are requested only if you enable the reminder feature.
  • Countdown Studio's optional public channel-statistics feature uses a YouTube Data API key entered by the visitor in the browser. Restrict your own key as recommended by Google and do not share private credentials.
  • The optional high-fidelity Word Converter route is present in the website source. If it is enabled and correctly configured for the page you are using, it sends the selected DOCX file to an external conversion provider through a Worker after an explicit in-page consent action. Live availability and domain configuration are not yet confirmed. The provider's own processing and retention terms apply.
  • QR Scanner can request camera access only after you choose Start Camera. Decoding of an uploaded QR image is performed in the browser using a third-party library; camera frames are not intentionally sent to an InfoJano processing server by the inspected page code.
