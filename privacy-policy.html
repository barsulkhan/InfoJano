INFOJANO · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InfoJano ("InfoJano", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit infojano.com and use its online tools. Different tools use different technologies. Please read the notice shown beside a tool before sending information to an external service.

Important: Many tools perform calculations or process files inside your browser. This is not a promise that every feature is fully local. The optional high-fidelity Word-to-PDF workflow sends a DOCX file to a third-party conversion service only after you choose that option and confirm its upload consent.

1. Information you provide

InfoJano does not require you to create an account to use its public tools. Depending on the tool, you may enter text, numbers, dates, URLs, contact details, images or documents. Avoid entering confidential, financial, government-ID or other sensitive information unless you have checked the relevant tool's processing notice and are comfortable with it.

For tools that run locally, inputs and generated results are processed in your browser and are not intentionally sent to an InfoJano application server. Some optional settings or history may be saved in your browser's local storage. You can clear browser site data to remove locally stored values.

2. Optional third-party document conversion

The optional high-fidelity DOCX conversion route in Word Converter requires you to enter a Worker endpoint and explicitly tick an upload-consent control before conversion. When you use that route, the selected DOCX is sent from your browser to the Worker endpoint you configured. The Worker is designed to relay the file to the iLoveAPI Office-to-PDF service for processing and return the result. InfoJano does not control the third-party provider's systems, security, or retention periods. Review the provider's current terms and privacy information before use. Do not upload confidential or sensitive documents unless you have independently assessed the risks.

The browser-based preview/export route is separate from this optional high-fidelity route. Read the instructions displayed in Word Converter to understand which route you are selecting.

3. Analytics and technical information

Some pages use Google Analytics 4. When enabled, Google may process information such as page views, interaction events, browser and device details, approximate location derived from network information, and identifiers or cookies, subject to the configuration and controls in the relevant Google service. We use analytics information to understand site usage and improve tools. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards.

4. Advertising and cookies

InfoJano has Google AdSense publisher code configured and may display advertising if the site and account are approved and ads are enabled. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to collect information as a result of ad serving. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other websites.

Learn how Google uses data on partner sites at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services. You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You may also visit aboutads.info for additional advertising opt-out information.

Cookies and similar technologies may also be used for essential site functionality, preferences, analytics and advertising, depending on the page and service. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect some site features.

5. Consent choices and regional requirements

Where applicable law or Google's publisher policies require consent for cookies, local storage or personalized advertising, the relevant consent choices must be presented through an appropriate consent mechanism. For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires publishers serving personalized ads to use a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). The EEA includes EU Member States, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.

InfoJano's implementation is being prepared to use Google's own certified CMP through AdSense Privacy & messaging. The consent message and its availability depend on the publisher account configuration, the site's AdSense tag and the visitor's region. Do not assume that a consent dialog is active merely because this policy describes it; the live message must be published and tested before personalized ads are served in these regions.

Google's documentation states that Privacy & messaging European regulations messages support IAB TCF v2.4. The IAB's deadline for full TCF v2.4 implementation by publishers and CMPs was 23 October 2026; publishers should verify that their selected CMP and consent flow meet the current requirements. The applicable CMP must continue to meet Google's current certification and TCF requirements. See Google's publisher CMP requirements, Google's CMP setup guide and Privacy & messaging guidelines.

Users should be able to review or change their consent choices through the consent message or the controls made available by Google. We will configure advertising and analytics consent signals according to the choices collected and the available AdSense settings. The availability and behavior of ads may depend on your location and the choices you make.

6. Third-party services and external links

Some tools load libraries, APIs or services operated by other providers. When a tool connects to an external service, that provider may receive technical information or the data needed to perform the requested operation. External websites have their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their practices. Tool-specific notices should explain when an operation leaves your browser.

7. Data retention and security

InfoJano does not operate a user account system for the public tools. Data stored in your browser remains subject to your browser and device settings. Data sent to an external provider is subject to that provider's systems, policies and retention practices. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure; use the tools with appropriate care.

8. Children's privacy

InfoJano's general-purpose tools are not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through a channel connected with the site, please contact us using the support route below so the situation can be reviewed.

9. Your choices

  • Use browser settings to manage or clear cookies and local storage.
  • Use Google Ads Settings to manage personalized advertising.
  • Do not use optional third-party upload features unless you accept the provider and processing route.
  • Use the Contact page to report a privacy concern. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when tools, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the InfoJano Contact page. The currently available public support route is the project's GitHub issue tracker; do not include private or sensitive information in a public report.
