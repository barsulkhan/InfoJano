Local time and date accuracy

This page is intended for people planning events in Bangladesh. Bangladesh Standard Time (BST, UTC+06:00) is used nationwide; Bangladesh does not currently observe daylight saving time. Before starting, enter the event's correct local date and time and confirm the timezone setting shown in Countdown Studio. For events announced in another region, verify the organiser's official time before sharing your countdown.
