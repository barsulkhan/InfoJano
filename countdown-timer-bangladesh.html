Local time and date accuracy

This page is intended for people planning events in Bangladesh. Bangladesh Standard Time (BST, UTC+06:00) is used nationwide; Bangladesh does not currently observe daylight saving time. Before starting, enter the event's correct local date and time and confirm the timezone setting shown in Countdown Studio. For events announced in another region, verify the organiser's official time before sharing your countdown.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use a countdown timer in Bangladesh?

Open InfoJano Countdown Studio, enter the event name, date and time, confirm Bangladesh Standard Time (UTC+06:00), then start your countdown.

Can I create an Eid countdown?

Yes. Enter the locally confirmed Eid date and time. Since the date may depend on moon sighting and official announcements, verify it before sharing.

Can the countdown show Bengali event names?

You can enter your own event name and message in the Countdown Studio. Bengali text can be used if supported by your device font.

Can I use it for exam dates and results?

Yes. Enter the date and time announced by the school, university or examination authority.

Will it work on a large TV?

You can open the full-screen countdown in a browser on a computer connected to a TV. Device and browser fullscreen support may vary.
