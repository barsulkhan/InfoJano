How to create it

  1. Open the QR Code Generator.
  2. Choose UPI Pay and enter your correct UPI ID.
  3. Add the payee name and, if needed, an optional amount and note.
  4. Generate the QR code and scan it using a trusted UPI app. Verify the payee details before paying.

Important checks before sharing

  • Check every phone number, account ID or destination before generating the QR code.
  • Scan the finished QR code with another phone and confirm that the displayed details are correct.
  • Use a clear, high-contrast print and leave a quiet white border around the QR code.
  • Never use a QR code as proof that a payment has been completed. Verify payments in your trusted payment app or bank account.

InfoJano only creates the QR image. It does not collect money, initiate a transaction or confirm payment. Always verify the recipient and payment status in your UPI app.

Frequently asked questions

Does this QR code receive money automatically?

No. It encodes payment details. The payer must scan it, review the details and approve payment in their UPI app.

Can I include a fixed amount?

The generator supports an optional amount. Check the amount and payee details carefully before displaying the QR.

Does InfoJano verify my UPI ID?

No. Confirm that your UPI ID is correct and active with your payment provider.
