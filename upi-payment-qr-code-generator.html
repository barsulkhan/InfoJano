How to create a UPI QR code

  1. Enter the UPI ID exactly as provided by your bank or payment app.
  2. Add an optional payee name and amount if the form offers those fields.
  3. Generate the QR, scan it with a trusted UPI app and verify the displayed payee details before displaying it.

Payment safety checklist

  • InfoJano creates the QR image; it does not receive, route or confirm payments.
  • Verify the UPI ID and payee name with the account holder before printing.
  • Never treat a scan or screenshot as proof of payment; check the credit in your own UPI app or bank account.
  • Do not approve collect requests or enter a UPI PIN to receive money.

InfoJano only creates the QR image. It does not collect money, initiate a transaction, verify a UPI ID or confirm payment. Always verify incoming funds in your own bank or UPI app.

Frequently asked questions

Does this QR code receive money automatically?

No. It encodes payment details. The payer must scan it, review the payee and amount, then approve the payment in their UPI app.

Can I include a fixed amount?

The generator supports an optional amount. Check the amount and payee details carefully before displaying the QR.

Does InfoJano verify my UPI ID or payment?

No. Confirm your UPI ID with your payment provider and verify incoming funds in your own bank or UPI app.
