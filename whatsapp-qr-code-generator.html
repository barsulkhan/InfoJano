Important checks before sharing

  • Check every phone number, account ID or destination before generating the QR code.
  • Scan the finished QR code with another phone and confirm that the displayed details are correct.
  • Use a clear, high-contrast print and leave a quiet white border around the QR code.
  • Never use a QR code as proof that a payment has been completed. Verify payments in your trusted payment app or bank account.

A WhatsApp QR code opens a chat destination. It does not send a message automatically; the person scanning it chooses whether to send.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a message to a WhatsApp QR code?

Yes. The generator supports an optional pre-filled message. The person scanning the code can review and send it.

Should I include the country code?

Yes. Use the full international phone number format and omit spaces or punctuation if the form requests digits only.

Is this an official WhatsApp service?

No. InfoJano creates a standard QR code containing a WhatsApp chat link. WhatsApp controls its own app and services.
