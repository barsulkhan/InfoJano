Helpful answers

WhatsApp QR code FAQs

What is a WhatsApp QR code?

It is a QR code that contains a WhatsApp chat link. When someone scans it, their device can open a conversation with the encoded number.

Can I create a WhatsApp QR without saving a number?

Yes. A WhatsApp Click to Chat link can open a conversation without the visitor first saving the number in their contacts.

Can I add a pre-filled message?

Yes. You can add an optional message in the generator. The person scanning the QR can review and send it; it is not sent automatically.

Is this WhatsApp QR generator free?

Yes. InfoJano's WhatsApp QR page is free to use and does not require an account.

Can I use the QR code for my shop or business?

Yes. You can place it on a shop counter, product card, business card, poster or website. Always scan-test it before printing.

Does a WhatsApp QR code expire?

A static QR code does not have a built-in expiry date, but it will only work while the encoded number and WhatsApp link remain valid.
