## Page mapping rules
- Do not create a separate page for every spelling or word-order variation.
- Group close variants on one genuinely useful page that satisfies the same intent.
- Each landing page must contain a working tool or a clearly useful workflow, unique instructions and FAQs; avoid doorway/thin pages.
- Use descriptive titles, concise meta descriptions, one clear H1, descriptive URLs, canonical URLs, internal links and sitemap entries.
- Keep all public-facing copy in English. Global-first; do not create Bengali/Hindi keyword pages.
- Do not add a meta keywords tag as a ranking tactic. Put terms naturally in visible copy and page metadata.
- Never claim unsupported features, scan guarantees, payment processing, dynamic tracking or analytics.
- Search-volume, competition and traffic estimates remain unverified until checked in first-party/keyword tools.
