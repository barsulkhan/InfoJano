About Ayushman Card printing

The Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) card is an official beneficiary document. InfoJano is an independent print-preparation utility: it does not issue Ayushman cards, check eligibility or change beneficiary records.

How to prepare your Ayushman Card for printing

  1. Download your own Ayushman card from the official PM-JAY beneficiary service or official Ayushman app.
  2. Open the InfoJano ID Card Print Studio and choose Ayushman Bharat Card.
  3. Upload the downloaded PDF or clear card image. JPG and PNG images can use the existing automatic split workflow; PDF pages are kept available for manual front and back cropping.
  4. Review the complete front and back, including every printed edge and all details. Adjust the crop if needed.
  5. Check the print preview and create the PDF or JPG. The current Ayushman preset uses 85 × 55 mm card output at 300 DPI and a 4 × 6 inch page layout.
Privacy and accuracy: Check that the full card, every printed detail and any QR code are visible before saving. InfoJano is a print-preparation utility only. It does not create official identity documents, change their details or replace the issuing authority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I print an Ayushman card from a PDF?

Yes. Upload the PDF and use the crop canvas to select the complete front and back card areas. Review both previews before exporting.

Does the tool issue or download an official Ayushman card?

No. Download the official beneficiary card from the authorised PM-JAY service first. InfoJano only helps prepare a file you already have for printing.

Can I use a JPG or PNG photo?

Yes. The current Ayushman workflow supports JPG and PNG images and has an automatic split path. Always inspect the front and back previews before saving.

Are my files uploaded to InfoJano?

The card processing is performed in your browser; the page does not intentionally send your selected card file to an InfoJano upload service. Use a trusted device and avoid sharing downloaded files.
