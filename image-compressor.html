How to Compress an Image

  1. Upload your image from your phone or computer.
  2. Choose a target size such as 20 KB or 100 KB.
  3. Click Compress Image and let Smart Compress work.
  4. Download your optimized image.

How to Reduce Image File Size

Choose an image, adjust the available compression settings, and preview the result before saving it. The final file size depends on image format, dimensions, detail and compression level.

Keep the image usable

  • Keep a copy of the original file.
  • Check text, faces and fine details after compression.
  • For a form upload, confirm the file-size and format limits on the destination website.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why compress an image?

Compression can reduce file size for uploads, sharing, and storage.

Will compression reduce image quality?

Lossy compression can reduce quality; preview the result and keep the original if quality matters.

Is there a maximum upload size?

Limits depend on your browser and device memory. If processing fails, try a smaller image.
