PDF Page Extractor – FAQs

How can I extract specific pages from a PDF?

Upload your PDF, select the page thumbnails or enter a range such as 2-5, 8, 10-12, then choose Extract selected pages.

Can I save selected PDF pages as one PDF?

Yes. InfoJano combines your selected pages in their original order into one new PDF document.

Can I extract odd or even pages?

Yes. Use the Quick selection menu to select all odd pages or all even pages, then adjust the selection if needed.

Will extracted pages lose quality?

No page is rendered as an image. The extractor copies original PDF page content, preserving selectable text and vector elements where supported by the source PDF.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

The extraction runs locally in your browser. Your document is not uploaded to an InfoJano processing server. The PDF library is loaded from a public CDN.
