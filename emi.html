What is EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed monthly payment made towards a loan, including principal and interest.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly payments.

EMI Example: What Changes the Monthly Payment?

An EMI estimate depends on the loan principal, annual interest rate and number of monthly instalments. Increasing the tenure often lowers the monthly instalment but may increase total interest paid.

Use the estimate carefully

  • Enter the annual interest rate as a percentage.
  • Enter tenure in years, as requested by the Loan Tenure (Years) field.
  • Compare total repayment as well as monthly EMI; lender fees and actual terms may differ.

EMI Calculator FAQs

What does EMI mean?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment: a regular monthly loan payment.

What affects my EMI?

Loan principal, interest rate, and repayment tenure affect the calculated EMI.

Is the result a bank offer?

No. It is an estimate; lender fees, rate changes, and loan terms may change the actual payment.
