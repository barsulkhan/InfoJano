What is EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed monthly payment made towards a loan, including principal and interest.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly payments.
