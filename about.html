ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About InfoJano

InfoJano is a collection of free online tools designed to help people complete everyday digital tasks. The site brings together calculators, date and time utilities, image and document tools, QR-code tools, text helpers and number converters.

What the site is for

Our aim is to make common tasks easier to complete through clear interfaces and practical explanations. Each tool page should describe what the tool does, explain important assumptions and limitations, and provide instructions that help visitors use the result correctly.

How tools work

Many tools are designed to process inputs in your browser. Some optional features may connect to third-party services, such as the explicitly consented high-fidelity Word document conversion route. The relevant tool page and our Privacy Policy explain these distinctions. Do not assume that every tool uses the same processing method.

Accuracy and limitations

Calculator outputs are intended as general-purpose estimates or arithmetic assistance, not professional, legal, medical, tax, payroll or financial advice. Check the assumptions and source information relevant to your situation before relying on a result.

Feedback and corrections

We welcome reports about broken tools, unclear instructions, accessibility issues and factual corrections. Visit our Contact page to reach the public support channel.
