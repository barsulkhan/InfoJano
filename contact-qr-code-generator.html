How to create it

  1. Open the QR Maker with Contact mode selected.
  2. Enter contact details in MECARD format, for example name, phone and email fields.
  3. Generate the QR code and scan it with a second phone.
  4. Check the decoded contact details before printing or sharing.

Safety and quality checks

  • Check the content and destination carefully before generating or sharing the QR code.
  • Scan the finished QR code with another phone and confirm the decoded information is correct.
  • Use strong contrast, a clear quiet border and a suitable print size.
  • Do not encode passwords, private data or payment instructions unless you understand who can access the QR content.

A QR code stores information; it does not guarantee that a link, phone number, email or payment destination is trustworthy. Review the result before taking action.

Frequently asked questions

What is MECARD?

MECARD is a text format used by some QR scanners to represent contact information.

Will every phone save the contact automatically?

Behavior varies by device and scanner. Some phones show a contact preview; users may need to choose Save.

What details should I include?

Include only details you intend to share, such as a name, business phone and public email.
