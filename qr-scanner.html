How to scan a QR code online

  1. Allow camera access only when you tap Start Camera, or upload a clear photo.
  2. Keep the entire QR code visible and in focus.
  3. Read the decoded text and check the destination carefully.
  4. Copy the result or send it to the QR generator if you want to create a new code.

Camera scanning requires a secure HTTPS connection and browser camera support. The image reader uses the ZXing library as a fallback.
