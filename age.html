Free Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to calculate your exact age in years, months and days.

Enter as DD-MM-YYYY — DD = day, MM = month, YYYY = 4-digit year. Examples: 15-08-1947 or 15081947 both mean 15 August 1947. Enter day first, then month, then the 4-digit year.

Manual entry only: DD-MM-YYYY (day/month/year), for example 15-08-1947.

How to calculate your age

Enter your date of birth manually in DD-MM-YYYY format and tap Calculate My Age. The calculator compares your birth date with today's date and shows your age in years, months and days.

Age Calculator FAQs

How is exact age calculated?

Enter your date of birth. The calculator compares it with today’s date and reports years, months and days.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

This calculator currently calculates your age as of today.

What does DD-MM-YYYY mean?

DD means day, MM means month, and YYYY means the four-digit year. For example, 15-08-1947 means 15 August 1947.
