Free Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to calculate your exact age in years, months and days.

Enter as DD/MM/YYYY — DD = day, MM = month, YYYY = 4-digit year. Example: 15/01/1995 means 15 January 1995. Write the day first, then month, then year.

You can type the date above, or select it below.

How to calculate your age

Select your birth date and tap Calculate My Age. The calculator compares your birth date with today's date and shows your age in years, months and days.

Age Calculator FAQs

How is exact age calculated?

Enter your date of birth. The calculator compares it with today’s date and reports years, months and days.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

This calculator currently calculates your age as of today.

What does DD/MM/YYYY mean?

DD means day, MM means month, and YYYY means the four-digit year. For example, 15/01/1995 means 15 January 1995.
