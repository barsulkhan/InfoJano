How to calculate your exact age

Enter your date of birth in DD-MM-YYYY format and tap Calculate My Age. This free online age calculator compares your birth date with today's date and shows your exact age in years, months and days, along with total days and full weeks.

Find your birth weekday and date history

The result also tells you the day of the week you were born. The “What Happened on Your Date?” feature looks for historical events recorded for the same day, month and birth year. If no matching event is available, that does not mean nothing happened; historical databases can be incomplete. Events from other years are labelled separately.

Age and Birthday Weekday Calculator FAQs

How can I find the day of the week I was born?

Enter your date of birth in DD-MM-YYYY format and tap Calculate My Age. The result shows whether you were born on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, or another day of the week.

How is exact age calculated?

Enter your date of birth. The calculator compares it with today’s date and reports years, months and days.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

This calculator currently calculates your age as of today.

What does DD-MM-YYYY mean?

DD means day, MM means month, and YYYY means the four-digit year. For example, 15-08-1947 means 15 August 1947.

Can I see historical events from my birth date?

Yes. After calculating your age, check “What Happened on Your Date?” The feature tries to show events recorded for the exact day, month and birth year, using available public history sources. Coverage varies by date, so a missing result does not prove that no event occurred. Events from other years are shown separately.
