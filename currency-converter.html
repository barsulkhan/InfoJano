How currency conversion works

Converted amount = original amount × exchange rate. The result updates when you change the amount or currency.

About exchange rates

Rates move throughout the day. The displayed quote is the latest data returned by our rate provider, not a guaranteed transaction price.

Before you make a payment

Banks, cards and transfer services may add fees or use their own exchange rates. Confirm the final amount with your provider.

Currency Converter FAQs

How often are exchange rates updated?

The rate provider publishes periodic updates. The page displays the provider's latest available timestamp and refreshes when requested.

Are these rates guaranteed by my bank?

No. These are indicative reference rates. Banks, card networks and money-transfer services may use different rates and fees.

Can I convert currencies other than USD and INR?

Yes. Choose from the available currency list. The rate service supports a broad range of world currencies.
