How Currency Conversion Works

Multiply the amount by the exchange rate to estimate its value in another currency.

Formula: Converted Amount = Original Amount × Exchange Rate.

Actual bank or money-transfer amounts may differ because of fees and provider rates.

Currency Converter FAQs

How do I convert one currency to another?

Choose the source and target currencies, enter an amount, and calculate the converted value.

Are exchange rates always live?

Rates may change frequently. Check the rate source and timestamp before using a conversion for a payment.

Will my bank give exactly this amount?

Not necessarily. Banks and payment providers may apply their own rates, fees, or spreads.
