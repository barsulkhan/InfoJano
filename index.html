What Is BMI?

BMI is a simple screening number based on weight and height.

Read guide

BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. It is useful for a quick estimate, but it does not describe every aspect of health.

A discount reduces the original price by a percentage or a fixed amount.
