Read guide

How EMI Is Calculated

EMI depends mainly on the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure.

Read guide

The monthly EMI is calculated using the standard reducing-balance loan formula. Change the amount, rate or tenure in the EMI tool to compare payments.

What Is BMI?

BMI is a simple screening number based on weight and height.

Read guide

BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. It is useful for a quick estimate, but it does not describe every aspect of health.

How Percentage Works

Percentage means “per hundred” and is useful for marks, prices, growth and comparisons.

Read guide

To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. Use the Percentage tool for quick calculations.

How Discount Works

A discount reduces the original price by a percentage or a fixed amount.

Read guide

Discount amount = original price × discount percentage ÷ 100. Sale price = original price − discount amount.

What Is a QR Code?

A QR code stores information that a phone camera can quickly scan.

Read guide

You can use a QR code for a website, text or other supported information. Use InfoJano QR Maker to create one quickly.
