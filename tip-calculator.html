How to Calculate a Tip

1. Calculate the Tip

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage, then divide by 100.

Tip = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

2. Find the Total Bill

Add the tip amount to the original bill to get the total payment.

Total = Bill Amount + Tip
