How to Calculate a Tip

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage and divide by 100. Add the tip to the bill to find the total payment.

Tip = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100.

Total = Bill Amount + Tip.

This calculator estimates the tip you enter. Local tipping customs and service charges vary.
