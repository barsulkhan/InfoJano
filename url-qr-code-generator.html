How to create it

  1. Copy the complete website address, including https://.
  2. Open the QR Maker. The Link / URL mode is selected automatically.
  3. Paste the address and generate your QR code.
  4. Download PNG, SVG or another available image format, then scan-test it before printing.

Safety and quality checks

  • Check the content and destination carefully before generating or sharing the QR code.
  • Scan the finished QR code with another phone and confirm the decoded information is correct.
  • Use strong contrast, a clear quiet border and a suitable print size.
  • Do not encode passwords, private data or payment instructions unless you understand who can access the QR content.

A QR code stores information; it does not guarantee that a link, phone number, email or payment destination is trustworthy. Review the result before taking action.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a QR code for any website?

Yes. Use a complete http or https website address. Check that the page is public and works on mobile.

Will the QR code change if my website changes?

The QR code stores the address you enter. If the page address changes, you may need to create and share a new QR code.

Can I print a URL QR code?

Yes. Download a high-resolution image, keep a clear border and test a printed sample before making many copies.
