## Executive summary
Build a topic cluster around practical use cases rather than publishing many near-duplicate country pages. Initial country pages:

## Competitor review: themes and product patterns
### Timeanddate
- Separate tool and event hubs: timers, stopwatch, countdown to any date, New Year countdown, popular holiday countdowns.
- Countdown creation includes title, date/time, location, display units, themes, shareable URL and fullscreen.
- It documents DST/location accuracy, final-seconds demo, and URL-based saving/sharing.
- Opportunity for InfoJano: simpler creation flow, large black display, final 10-second animation, event-specific celebration and personal message.
Sources: [create countdown](https://www.timeanddate.com/countdown/create), [countdown help](https://www.timeanddate.com/countdown/help), [timers overview](https://www.timeanddate.com/counters/).

### TickCounter
- Custom event countdown with timezone, name, font, link, units, language, dimensions/layout, background, finish behaviour, share link and embed code.
- Positions itself around simple creation, responsive use, multilingual support and embedding.
- Opportunity: emphasize free, no-signup personal countdown plus fullscreen celebration; make sharing and mobile/TV use clear.
Sources: [TickCounter homepage](https://www.tickcounter.com/), [about/features](https://www.tickcounter.com/info/about).

### Online Stopwatch
- Large family of intent-specific tools: classroom timers, exam timers, presentation timers, holiday timers, dates/birthdays, large stopwatch, fullscreen countdown, video timers and custom timers.
- Opportunity: create focused SEO pages only where InfoJano provides a matching tool experience; avoid pages that merely repeat the same generic timer.
Sources: [Online Stopwatch homepage](https://www.online-stopwatch.com/), [tool directory](https://www.online-stopwatch.com/online-stopwatch/).

### Additional competitor set
- countdown.online, countdownpage.com, mycountdown.com, timer-tab.com, online-timer.com, countdown.io and countingdownto.com appear in current public recommendation/competitor roundups. Treat the roundup as discovery, not authoritative traffic/rank data.
Source: [countdown timer competitor roundup](https://aibrandtracker.keywordseverywhere.com/prompt-category/countdown-timer).

## Recommended content architecture
1. Pillar: `countdown-studio.html` — free online countdown timer / event countdown / full-screen timer.
2. Existing supporting page: `birthday-countdown-calculator.html` — birthday-specific day counter; keep distinct from event countdown creation.
3. Country pages (Phase 1): India and Bangladesh.
4. Later event pages only with distinct functionality or substantial unique content: exam countdown, wedding countdown, New Year countdown, Eid countdown, classroom/presentation countdown.
5. Country expansion candidates after exact demand validation: Pakistan, UAE, UK, USA.

## On-page and technical checklist for the first two pages
- Unique title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph and visible country-specific content.
- WebPage, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage JSON-LD matching visible content.
- Clear CTA to Countdown Studio; internal link to birthday calculator.
- Add to sitemap.xml, sitemap-infojano.xml, sitemap.txt and Service Worker precache.
- Avoid false claims about official festival dates, automatic holiday detection, background reminders or timezone auto-conversion.
- Search Console indexing is a separate step after Pages deployment; do not claim indexed based on sitemap inclusion.

## Competitor keyword themes to follow
- Core function: online countdown timer, countdown to date, countdown clock.
- Display: full screen countdown timer, large screen countdown, classroom timer, presentation timer.
- Occasion: birthday countdown, wedding countdown, holiday countdown, New Year countdown, Christmas countdown.
- Use case: exam countdown, event countdown, launch countdown, deadline countdown.
- Product features: share countdown link, custom countdown, countdown with animation, embed countdown.
