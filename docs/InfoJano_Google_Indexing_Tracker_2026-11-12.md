- Whenever new tool pages, toolkit pages, or SEO landing pages are created or published for InfoJano, add their canonical URLs to the **Pending URLs** section immediately.
- Newly created pages must be treated as **Pending — not submitted** until they are added to a batch and submitted in Google Search Console.
- Keep newly added URLs in the pending queue even if they were not part of the sitemap snapshot used for the original 88-URL count.
- Before preparing each new 10-URL batch, refresh the sitemap and include all newly published pages that are still not verified as indexed.
- Do not remove a URL from Pending or mark it Indexed until Google Search Console verifies indexing.

## Batch workflow
1. Submit only the 10 URLs in the active batch for indexing.
2. After Google Search Console confirms their indexing, mark those URLs Indexed with the verification date.
3. Only then prepare the next batch of up to 10 from the pending list.
4. Never mark a URL indexed based only on submission or a crawl request.
5. Refresh this tracker against the latest sitemap before issuing later batches.
