Local time and date accuracy

This page is intended for people planning events in India. India uses Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+05:30) nationwide and does not observe daylight saving time. Before starting, enter the event's correct local date and time and confirm the timezone setting shown in Countdown Studio. For events announced in another region, verify the organiser's official time before sharing your countdown.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a countdown timer in India?

Open Countdown Studio, enter your event name, choose its date and time, confirm Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+05:30), then start and share your countdown.

Does this countdown use Indian Standard Time?

The page is intended for events in India. Confirm the timezone shown in the Countdown Studio form and use IST (UTC+05:30) for India-based events.

Can I make a birthday or wedding countdown?

Yes. Enter the date and time of the birthday, wedding or anniversary and start a personal countdown.

Can I show the countdown on a TV?

Yes. Open the countdown on a computer connected to the TV and use the full-screen display. Actual browser and TV behaviour can vary by device.

Does InfoJano automatically know festival dates?

No. Enter and verify the date from an official or trusted calendar. Festival dates can vary by region or lunar-calendar announcement.
