Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100.

How do I find the sale price?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price.

How much do I save on a 20% discount?

On an original price of 1000, you save 200 and pay 800 before extra taxes or charges.

Can I compare two discounts?

Calculate each final price using the same original price to compare the savings.

Discount Calculator Examples

For an original price of 1,000 and a 15% discount, you save 150 and pay 850 before extra taxes or charges. For a quick example, a 20% discount on 100 saves 20, leaving a sale price of 80.

How to calculate a discount

Discount amount = original price × discount percentage ÷ 100. Sale price = original price − discount amount.

Compare offers fairly

  • Use the same original price for each offer.
  • Check whether delivery charges or taxes are added later.
  • Compare the final payable price, not only the advertised percentage.
