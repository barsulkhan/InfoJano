How to Calculate a Discount

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100 to find your savings. Subtract the savings from the original price to find the final price.

Discount amount = Original price × Discount percentage ÷ 100

Final price = Original price − Discount amount

Example: If an item costs 1000 and has a 20% discount, you save 200 and pay 800.

Discount Calculator FAQs

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100.

How do I find the sale price?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price.

Can I compare two discounts?

Calculate each final price using the same original price to compare the savings.
