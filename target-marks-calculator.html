Target Marks Calculator: Formula

Additional marks needed = (Total marks × Target percentage ÷ 100) − Marks already obtained

Worked example

For 500 total marks, an 80% target means 400 marks. If you already have 350, you need 50 more marks.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the total possible marks.
  2. Enter the marks you have already obtained.
  3. Enter the target percentage and calculate the additional marks needed.

Common questions

How many marks do I need for 80 percent?

Multiply the total marks by 80 and divide by 100. For 500 total marks, 80% is 400 marks.

Can I find how many more marks are needed?

Yes. Enter total marks, marks already scored and the target percentage. The calculator subtracts your current marks from the target marks.

What if I have already reached my target?

The additional marks needed will be zero or a negative value depending on the current score.
