Frequently asked questions

How do I create a countdown timer in the USA?

Open InfoJano Countdown Studio, enter the event name, date and time, confirm the local time zone for the event location, then start and share your countdown.

How do I choose the correct US time zone?

The United States spans several time zones. Use the event's local time for its city or region and account for daylight saving time where applicable.

Can I create a birthday, wedding or holiday countdown?

Yes. Set the date and time for a birthday, wedding, Thanksgiving, Halloween, Christmas, Independence Day or another personal event.

Can I show the countdown on a TV?

Open the countdown on a computer connected to the TV and use the full-screen display. Actual browser and TV behaviour can vary by device.

Does InfoJano automatically know US holiday dates?

No. Enter and verify the date from an official calendar or event organiser. Some holidays have fixed dates while others vary by year or observance.
