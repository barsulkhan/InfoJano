Local time and date accuracy

The United States spans Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaska and Hawaii time zones, with additional local exceptions. Daylight saving time is observed in most regions but not everywhere. Set the countdown using the event's local city or region time and verify the date/time before sharing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a countdown timer in the USA?

Open InfoJano Countdown Studio, enter the event name, date and time, confirm the event's local time zone, then start and share your countdown.

How do I choose the correct US time zone?

The United States has multiple time zones. Use the event's local time for its city or region and account for daylight saving time where applicable.

Can I create a birthday, wedding or holiday countdown?

Yes. Enter the date and time for a birthday, wedding, Thanksgiving, Halloween, Christmas or another event.

Can I show the countdown on a TV?

Open the countdown on a computer connected to the TV and use the full-screen display. Actual browser and TV behaviour can vary by device.

Does InfoJano automatically know US holiday dates?

No. Enter and verify the date from an official calendar or event organiser. Some holidays have fixed dates while others vary by year or observance.
