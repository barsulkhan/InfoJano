US time zones for an accurate countdown

Choose the time zone for the place where the event will happen—not simply the time zone of the person creating the timer. Common US time-zone choices include:

  • Eastern Time: New York, Washington, DC and much of the East Coast.
  • Central Time: Chicago, Dallas and much of the central states.
  • Mountain Time: Denver and much of the Mountain region; local exceptions apply.
  • Pacific Time: Los Angeles, San Francisco and Seattle.
  • Alaska Time and Hawaii-Aleutian Time: use these for events in Alaska and Hawaii.

Most US regions observe daylight saving time, but not all locations do. Confirm the city or region's local date and time before sharing a countdown.
