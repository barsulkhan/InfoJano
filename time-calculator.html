Free Elapsed Time Calculator

Use this free elapsed time calculator to find exactly how much time passes between two clock times. Enter a start time and an end time to get the difference in hours and minutes, plus the total number of minutes.

How overnight calculation works

If the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator treats the end time as being on the next day. For example, 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8 hours.

A quick example

From 9:15 AM to 12:00 PM, the elapsed time is 2 hours and 45 minutes, or 165 minutes in total.

When it is useful

Check work shifts, study sessions, travel duration, appointments, exercise time, and the length of everyday activities.

For an accurate result, enter both times carefully. The calculator displays hours and minutes and also shows the total minutes, making it easy to record or compare durations.
