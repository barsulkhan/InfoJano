TIME MADE SIMPLE

Plan Your Time, One Calculation at a Time

Whether you are checking work hours, planning a study session or measuring time between two moments, InfoJano’s elapsed time calculator helps you turn start and end times into a clear duration.

◷Check a time difference

Enter when an activity starts and ends to see the total time spent. You can also subtract an unpaid break to find the net working duration.

↗Calculate across midnight

If an end time falls after midnight, the calculator can account for the overnight period. For example, 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8 hours.

＋Add or subtract time

Start with a clock time, add or subtract hours, minutes and seconds, and see whether the result falls on the previous or following day.

▦Compare full dates

Use Date + Time mode when both the calendar date and clock time matter. The result includes days, remaining time, total hours, minutes and seconds.

✦
Quick tip: For the most accurate result, check AM/PM and include seconds when they matter. For a full date range, use Date + Time mode rather than the clock-only difference mode.
