How many days old am I?

Enter your date of birth in the DD-MM-YYYY format on the Age Calculator. The tool compares your birth date with today or a reference date you select, then displays the total elapsed days. It also shows complete weeks and remaining days, hours and minutes based on whole calendar days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my age in days?

Enter your date of birth and calculate your age. The total-days result is the number of calendar days elapsed between the two dates.

Are leap years included?

Yes. The date difference uses actual calendar dates, so February 29 in leap years is included.

Can I calculate days up to another date?

Yes. Use the reference-date field in the Age Calculator to calculate your age on a past or future date, provided it is not before your birth date.
